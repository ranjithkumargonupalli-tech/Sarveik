About Us

Sarveik helps you find the right tools faster, and brings a friendly community along for the ride.

Who We Are

Sarveik is a curated directory of tools for students, teachers, developers, gamers, shoppers, music enthusiasts, and more. We help you find the right tool quickly, and we add a community layer on top so you can discover, rate, and share what works.

Our goal: make discovering great tools simple, social, and rewarding.

What We Offer

Tool Directory

Browse and search a curated collection of third‑party tools across many categories.

AI Assistant

Ask questions about tools and get quick answers to help you choose.

Credits & Referrals

Earn credits through daily login, referrals, and approved tool submissions.

Community

Build a profile, connect with friends, chat, rate tools, and save favorites.

How It Works

Discover

Search the directory or ask the AI assistant for suggestions.

Connect

Rate tools, save favorites, and chat with friends.

Earn

Collect credits for daily logins, referrals, and tool approvals.

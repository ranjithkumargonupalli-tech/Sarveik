Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 2026 Updated

Your privacy is our priority. This policy explains how Sarveik collects, uses, and protects your data.

Sarveik ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy applies to all services offered on our website, including tool directories, AI assistant, credit system, referrals, payments, and social features.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

Username, email, hashed password, profile picture, display name, bio, social links.

Social Login Data

Google ID, name, email (if you sign in via Google).

Usage Data

Tools you view/use, friend requests, messages, ratings, favorites, search queries.

Payment Information

We do not store card details. PhonePe processes payments; we store transaction reference and credits purchased.

AI Interactions

Your questions sent to Cohere API (third‑party) for generating responses.

Communication Data

Email address for notifications, support tickets, and weekly digests.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Authentication & Account Management: To create and manage your account, log you in, and remember your preferences.
  • Service Delivery: To display personalized tool recommendations, track your usage for streaks and credits, and enable friend requests / chat.
  • Credits & Payments: To award credits for referrals, daily login, tool approvals, and process credit purchases via PhonePe.
  • AI Assistant: To answer your questions about tools. Your messages are sent to Cohere’s API (see their privacy policy).
  • Notifications: To send you email updates (welcome, password change, weekly digest, support replies). You can opt out of non‑essential emails.
  • Analytics & Improvement: To analyze tool popularity, user engagement, and improve our platform.
  • Legal Compliance: To enforce our Terms of Service and comply with applicable laws.

3. Third‑Party Services

We use trusted third‑party services to operate Sarveik . Each service has its own privacy policy:

  • Google OAuth – for social login. We receive your name and email address. Google Privacy Policy
  • PhonePe – for credit purchases. We do not store your payment details. PhonePe Privacy Policy
  • Cohere AI – for AI chat assistant. Your questions are sent to Cohere. Cohere Privacy Policy
  • Nodemailer / Gmail – for sending transactional and promotional emails.
  • Microsoft SQL Server / MongoDB – database hosting (your data is stored on our secure servers).

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on secure servers with encryption at rest. Passwords are hashed using bcrypt. We use HTTPS for all data transmission. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. We take reasonable measures to protect your information but cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Cookies & Tracking

We use session cookies to keep you logged in. We also use analytics cookies to understand how you use our platform. You can disable cookies in your browser, but some features may not work properly.

We do not use third‑party advertising cookies.

6. Your Rights (GDPR / DPDPA)

Depending on your location (e.g., EU, India), you may have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of your personal data.
  • Right to Rectification: Correct inaccurate data.
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your account and associated data (via Profile → Delete Account).
  • Right to Data Portability: Export your data (we provide an export feature).
  • Right to Object / Restrict Processing: You can opt out of marketing emails.

To exercise your rights, contact us at ranjithkumar@sarveik.com or use the Profile page.

7. Children’s Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under 13 (or under 16 in some regions). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with data, please contact us.

8. Data Retention

We retain your account data as long as your account is active. Tool usage data may be anonymized after 1 year for analytics. You can delete your account at any time, which will erase most personal data except for anonymized usage statistics.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact:

  • Email: ranjithkumar@sarveik.com
  • DMCA / Copyright: ranjithkumar@sarveik.com
  • Support: ranjithkumar@sarveik.com

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. We will notify you of any material changes via email (if you have provided one) or by posting a notice on our website. The "Effective date" at the top indicates when this policy was last revised.

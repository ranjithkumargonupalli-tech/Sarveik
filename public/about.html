Who We Are

Sarveik (sarveik.com) is a curated directory of tools for students, teachers, developers, gamers, shoppers, music enthusiasts, and more. We help you find the right tool quickly, and we add a community layer on top so you can discover, rate, and share what works.

Our goal: make discovering great tools simple, social, and rewarding.

Why We Built Sarveik

The internet is full of useful tools, but finding the right one often means digging through endless search results, outdated lists, and ads. We built Sarveik to fix that: one friendly place where tools are organised by what you actually want to do, and where real people share what worked for them.

Good to Know

Does Sarveik own the tools listed in the directory?

No. The tools listed are third‑party products. Sarveik helps you discover them, but each tool belongs to its respective owner and has its own terms.

How do I earn credits?

You can earn credits through daily login, by referring friends, and when a tool you submit is approved.

How can I suggest a tool or report a problem?

Use the tool submission feature on the site, or email us at ranjithkumar@sarveik.com. Copyright concerns can be raised through our DMCA page.

Is Sarveik free to use?

You can browse the directory and join the community on sarveik.com. See our Terms of Service for the full details of using the platform.

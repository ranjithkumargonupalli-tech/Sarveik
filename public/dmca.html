DMCA & Contact
Information

Sarveik respects intellectual property rights. If you believe content on this site infringes your copyright, follow the procedure outlined below.

Notification of Infringement

To file a valid DMCA takedown notice, your submission must include all of the following:

  1. 01 Your full name, mailing address, and a valid electronic or physical signature.
  2. 02 Clear identification of the copyrighted work you claim has been infringed.
  3. 03 The specific URL or location of the allegedly infringing material on our platform.
  4. 04 Your contact information including a valid email address and phone number.
  5. 05 A good faith statement that the disputed use has not been authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  6. 06 A declaration, under penalty of perjury, that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner.

Incomplete notices may not be acted upon. Ensure all required elements are included before submitting to avoid delays in processing.

Contact Channels

Direct your DMCA notice or general inquiry to the appropriate channel below:

dmca@Sarveik.com

 General Support

We review all submissions and respond within 2–5 business days. Infringing material confirmed under a valid DMCA notice will be removed as required by applicable law.

Counter-Notification

If you believe material was removed as a result of a mistake or misidentification, you may file a counter-notification. Your counter-notice must include your contact details, identification of the removed material and its prior location, a statement under penalty of perjury that removal was a mistake, and your consent to jurisdiction of federal court.

Filing a false DMCA notice or counter-notice may expose you to civil liability. Consider consulting legal counsel before proceeding.
